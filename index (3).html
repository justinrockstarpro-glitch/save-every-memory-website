Your important records deserve more than a filing cabinet. We help turn paper documents into organized digital archives that are easier to search and retrieve.

Document Management & Digital Records Solutions

From a one-time digitization project to ongoing records support, we can help your team move from paper-heavy storage to a more accessible digital workflow. We scan, organize, index and help build searchable digital databases so your staff can find the records they need more easily.

  • High-volume document scanning and digitization
  • OCR processing to make supported scanned documents text-searchable
  • Organizing and indexing files into searchable digital databases for easier retrieval
  • Digitizing contracts, invoices, case files, historical records and more
  • One-time projects or ongoing scanning and indexing services

Available by custom quote. Project scope, file organization and delivery format are discussed before work begins.

Know what you have

Media formats we
preserve and digitize.

Not sure what type of tape, slide or film you have? Compare your media with the examples below, then contact us if you need help identifying it.

VHS

Full-size home video cassettes commonly used in VCRs.

VHS-C

Smaller camcorder tapes, often used with an adapter for VHS players.

Hi-8

Compact 8mm-family camcorder tapes, including Hi-8 recordings.

MiniDV

Small digital video cassettes used in many late-1990s and 2000s camcorders.

35mm Slides

Individual mounted transparencies, often viewed in a slide projector.

35mm Film & Negatives

Photographic film strips and negatives ready for scanning.
