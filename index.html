Know what you have

Media formats we
preserve and digitize.

Not sure what type of tape, slide or film you have? Compare your media with the examples below, then contact us if you need help identifying it.

VHS

Full-size home video cassettes commonly used in VCRs.

VHS-C

Smaller camcorder tapes, often used with an adapter for VHS players.

Hi-8

Compact 8mm-family camcorder tapes, including Hi-8 recordings.

MiniDV

Small digital video cassettes used in many late-1990s and 2000s camcorders.

35mm Slides

Individual mounted transparencies, often viewed in a slide projector.

35mm Film & Negatives

Photographic film strips and negatives ready for scanning.

These photos are visual examples. Tape labels, colors and cassette designs can vary by manufacturer and recording format.
